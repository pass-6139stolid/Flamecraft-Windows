## 🕹️ About

Flamecraft is a cozy turn-based strategy game developed by Monster Couch and based on the acclaimed tabletop game from Cardboard Alchemy. In a magical town, you become a Flamekeeper who helps artisan dragons find homes in shops that make coffee, cakes, potions, and other goods. Place dragons, gather resources, enchant shops, activate dragon abilities, and build a growing engine of reputation and rewards. Designed for one to five players, Flamecraft combines approachable card-game decisions, resource management, and charming fantasy presentation.

Flamecraft is made for players who enjoy relaxed strategy, colorful fantasy worlds, card-driven planning, and cozy games with gentle competition.

## ✨ Features

- 🏪 **Enchantable Shops** — Gather goods and enchant shops to unlock rewards, reputation, and new possibilities.
- ✨ **Whimsical Fantasy** — Explore a colorful shopping district filled with friendly dragons and magical businesses.
- 👥 **Solo and Multiplayer** — Play alone or compete with up to four other players through online and local multiplayer.
- 🔥 **Dragon Abilities** — Fire up the dragons in a shop to trigger helpful effects and extend your turns.
- 🃏 **Card-Based Strategy** — Combine shop cards, dragon cards, enchantments, and resources into efficient plays.
- 🐉 **Artisan Dragons** — Place specialized dragons in shops where their talents can improve the town.
